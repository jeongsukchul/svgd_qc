## 5. Cross-cutting findings (the mechanism story)
1. **Universal late-decay pathology.** On nearly every task the policy reaches a near-SOTA
   peak mid-run then decays: lift .94→.78, can .88→.64, pen-human 75→22, kitchen-mixed 58→47,
   door 11.9→0. Peaks track or beat TRQAM; endpoints don't.
2. **The decay is refine/critic drift, not decoder drift.** Freeze (bc_stop_step=600k) fully
   cures it only for base-anchored lift (0.908); with data anchors or on Adroit the decay
   continues through the frozen-decoder era (pen-human+freeze: peak 75 → 22).
   Large drift_adam_eps (soft freeze) improves early learning but does not stabilize late
   (and collapses can to 0.38). λ-warmup and prior-sampled bases were falsified earlier.
3. **Manifold metric ≈ implicit λ-scaling on these domains.** Scale-matched Euclidean (λ=3)
   ties or beats the full JJᵀ metric on lift (0.776/pk 0.96 vs 0.834/pk 0.94), can
   (0.660 vs 0.678), and **wins square outright (0.328 vs 0.260)**. Un-scale-matched
   Euclidean (λ=0.1) collapses (δ_rms 0.3 vs 0.02) — the metric's real function here is
   setting the effective trust-region strength.
4. **Anchor law flipped vs OGBench-cube:** data anchor wins can (+0.03), pen-human (+30 pts!),
   kitchen-mixed (+46 pts); base+freeze still wins lift. Another per-task switch, not a law.
5. **num_qs=2 vs TRQAM's K=10:** q10 helps square (+0.04) but does not close it.
6. **Time doesn't fix square:** 2M-step run is on the same plateau at its 1M midpoint.

## 6. Honest assessment
The algorithm is competitive at peak but loses at endpoints; nothing generic
(λ, γ, freeze, eps, ensemble, metric) has closed that. The single differentiated positive:
untuned-recipe wins on Adroit-cloned cells (pen-cloned 71.5 » IQL 37; hammer-cloned ≥ ReBRAC)
and pen-human data-anchor (63.0, peak 105). The live fix attempt is a **hinge trust-region**
(`delta_budget`): zero penalty inside a per-dim offset budget, stiff wall beyond — targeting
the diagnosed decay mechanism directly and replacing per-task λ. Probes (lift/can/square,
ε∈{0.01,0.04}) are queued/running; prediction: peaks hold to 1M.
